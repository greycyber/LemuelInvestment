Professional Logistics Support Since 2015

Moving Your Goods. Simplifying Your Logistics.

Lemuel Investment Ltd is a Ghanaian business established in 2015 by two business partners with an interest in the clearing, forwarding, and logistics sector.

Our activities focus on helping clients navigate the various stages involved in international cargo movement—from documentation and shipment coordination to customs processing, cargo release, and delivery.

We understand that international cargo movement requires coordination among multiple parties, including importers, exporters, shipping lines, customs authorities (GRA), terminals, and regulatory institutions. We combine practical logistics experience with professional knowledge to provide responsive and dependable support.

Comprehensive Clearing & Forwarding Solutions

We offer 20 core services across pre-arrival, customs clearance, port operations, and post-clearance logistics. Here are our primary focus areas:

Import Customs Clearance

Assistance with customs documentation, declaration, duty assessment, joint examination, and cargo-release processes at Ghanaian ports.

Export Customs Clearance

Support with export documentation, permits, customs processing, and coordination of shipments leaving Ghana by sea or air.

ICUMS Declaration Support

Lodging customs declarations electronically through the Integrated Customs Management System (ICUMS) accurately and efficiently.

Customs Documentation

Preparation, review, and verification of shipping documents, regulatory permits (FDA, GSA), and HS Code classification.

Freight Forwarding

Coordination of international cargo movement between suppliers, carriers, ports, and inland destinations, including transit cargo.

Transportation Coordination

Sourcing and coordinating reliable inland transportation from ports, airports, and freight stations directly to designated warehouses.

Our Process

How We Work: A streamlined 6-step approach to moving your goods.

01. Consultation

Understanding shipment, origin, destination & logistics needs.

02. Review

Reviewing commercial, shipping & regulatory documentation.

03. Processing

Coordinating ICUMS customs declarations & permits.

04. Verification

Coordinating examination, scanning, duties & taxes.

05. Release

Following up on requirements necessary for cargo release.

06. Delivery

Coordinating transportation to the final destination.
